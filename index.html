Hello, I am Xuefeng.

Building multimodal AI that
reasons more reliably.

I am a second-year Ph.D. student (D2) in the Natural Language Processing Laboratory at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST). I am advised by Taro Watanabe, Hidetaka Kamigaito, and Yusuke Sakai.

My research focuses on vision-language models, trustworthy AI, bias and fairness, and multimodal reasoning. I am especially interested in diagnosing where modern models fail and designing evidence-grounded evaluation methods for culturally rich, spatial, and high-stakes settings.
